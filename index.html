About

I want to make games that stay with players long after they stop playing.

I'm a game development student at ISART Digital in Nice, originally from Chamonix. I work in Unity and Godot, solo and in teams with designers and artists. Before games, I spent years in professional kitchens, including a Michelin-starred restaurant. That taught me rigor, working under time pressure and keeping a team moving.

Games that shaped how I think about design: Blue Prince, Mouthwashing, Omori, OneShot, Threshold and Mole. I'm especially inspired by Tonda Ros and Mike Klubnika.
